Director's Message

Words from our leadership

Dr. Kishor Saste

Director, Universal Education Consultant

Dear Students and Parents,

Education is the cornerstone of a bright and successful future, and choosing the right path—especially in the field of medicine—is one of the most important decisions you will ever make. With over 25 years of experience in guiding aspiring medical professionals, I am proud to say that Universal Education Consultant has helped thousands of students realize their dream of studying MBBS abroad in some of the world’s most prestigious and reputable universities.

Studying MBBS overseas not only offers access to world-class education and globally recognized degrees, but also provides exposure to advanced medical practices—all at an affordable cost. Countries such as Russia, Nepal, China, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Bangladesh, and many others offer high-quality education with English-medium instruction and internationally accredited medical programs.

At Universal Education Consultant, we are deeply committed to supporting students throughout their journey—from choosing the right university and securing admission, to visa processing and post-arrival support. Our dedicated team of experienced counselors and strong network of partner universities ensure that every student receives personalized guidance and a seamless experience.

Your dream of becoming a doctor deserves the right guidance and the right start. At Universal Education Consultant, we are here to walk every step of the way with you—because your success is our mission.

Warm regards,

Dr. Kishor Saste
